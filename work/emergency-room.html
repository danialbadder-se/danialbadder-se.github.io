Excel reporting · January 2024 view

Building the workbook

I organized patient visits into an Excel Data Model and used a calendar table to filter the report by month and year. PivotTables feed the patient count, average wait, satisfaction score, and breakdowns by admission, age, gender, and department.

The January view makes the busiest age group easy to see. Patients aged 60–69 accounted for 99 of the 513 visits. General Practice received 103 referrals, the most of any named department.
